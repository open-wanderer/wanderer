<objective>
Fix the non-optimistic reorder snap-back in `SettingsCategoriesScreen` and
`SettingsSubcategoriesScreen`. Today the `_dragging` guard is cleared in
`onReorderEnd`, which Flutter's `SliverReorderableList` fires synchronously
right after `onReorder` returns — before the `await reorder()` network call
resolves. That premature clear lets the next `build()` reseed `_orderedIds`
from the still-stale provider data, snapping the dragged item back to its
original slot until the server responds and the provider re-emits.

Extend the guard's protected window so it covers the entire drag→reorder()
round-trip: keep it set from `onReorderStart` through the `await reorder()`
call, clearing it only after the call resolves (success or caught failure)
inside `_onReorder`. Stop touching the guard in `onReorderEnd`.

Purpose: The optimistic reorder must remain visible from drop until the
provider catches up, eliminating the visible snap-back + late jump.
Output: Two edited Dart screens with a round-trip-scoped reorder guard,
WR-02 mid-drag protection intact, verified via `dart analyze`.
</objective>
