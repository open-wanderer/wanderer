<objective>
Provide the tooling and the end-of-phase verification checklist for the three headline behaviors of `TileRepositoryManager` that cannot be unit-tested because they require a live ObjectBox Store, a real network transfer, and real OS lifecycle signals: (1) HTTP Range resume from a partial `.part` file, (2) disk-space refusal, and (3) backgrounding-as-deliberate-pause — plus DEM independence and the `localTilePathsForBounds` query end-to-end. The research rates platform-specific disk-space and backgrounding semantics at MEDIUM confidence (docs, not on-device), so this on-device verification is the phase's real proof.

Purpose: The unit tests in Plans 02-05 cover every pure seam (`resumePlanFor`, `bboxOverlaps`, `hasEnoughSpace`, path-safety, enum round-trips); this plan builds an on-device driver harness and records the I/O-orchestration behaviors as a `<verify><human-check>` for the end-of-phase verification pass. This project runs `human_verify_mode: end-of-phase`, so there is no mid-flight `checkpoint:human-verify` task — the human confirmation happens at end-of-phase via the recorded human-check.
Output: A small on-device driver harness (`tile_repository_manager_harness.dart`) plus an explicit human-check listing the five behaviors to confirm on a real device against the now Range-forwarding backend.
</objective>
