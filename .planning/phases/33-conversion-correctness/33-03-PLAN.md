## Why `cumulativeDistance` is repaired, not deleted (D-01)

The ROADMAP's phrase "the dead, misaligned `cumulativeDistance` array is gone" describes a
*misaligned* array, not an unused one. 33-RESEARCH.md verified a live, wired consumer: the
trail-edit route-crop slider (`updateCropMarkers()` / `getCoordinateAtDistance()` in
`web/src/routes/trail/edit/[id]/+page.svelte`, driven by `RouteEditor.svelte`). Deleting the field
breaks a shipped feature. D-01 locks: rebuild in place as a raw, index-aligned per-point array,
decoupled from the reported total. `list_panel.svelte`'s identically-named local (lines 30 / 160)
is an unrelated concept — a sum of whole trails' `.distance` — do not touch it.

Measured shapes, verified against the real parser (post-33-01):

| Fixture | `flatten()` length | `cumulativeDistance` length today | after this plan |
|---------|--------------------|-----------------------------------|-----------------|
| 2-point segment | 2 | 1 | 2, first entry `0` |
| two-leg planner route | 6 | 5 | 6, first entry `0` |
| 16-point jitter track | 16 | 15 | 16, first entry `0` |
| empty segment | 0 | 0 | 0 |
