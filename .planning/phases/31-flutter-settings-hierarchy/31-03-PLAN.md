<objective>
Close the single minor UAT gap (31-UAT.md Test 1): the collapsible Settings hierarchy renders all ~1306 seeded CoMaps regions because the app never parses the backend's per-leaf `enabled` flag. Add `enabled` to `RegionHierarchyRow` and `RegionTreeNode`, add a pure `pruneToDownloadable` step that removes non-enabled leaves and any group left childless, and wire it into `_refreshCatalog` so the tree is pruned once per genuine fetch — before `flattenVisible` runs.

Purpose: Restore the intended end-user behavior — "only show the regions (and their parents) that are actually downloadable" — per the resolved product decision (filter on `enabled == true` only; keep enabled-but-still-building leaves; do NOT additionally filter on build status). The backend already sends `enabled` on every leaf row (`db/routes/regions_get.go:70`); this is entirely a client-side parse+prune gap, no Go change required.
Output: Two model edits (+ regenerated Freezed/JSON parts), one new pure function in `region_tree_util.dart`, a one-call wiring change in the screen, and extended unit + widget tests proving disabled leaves and now-childless groups are pruned while enabled leaves survive.
</objective>

## Gap being closed

- **truth:** "Settings -> Offline Maps/Regions shows the collapsible hierarchy with group and leaf rows matching the admin-defined tree" — failed because every seeded region renders, not only downloadable ones.
- **root_cause:** `RegionHierarchyRow`/`RegionTreeNode` never parse the leaf-only `enabled` flag (already on the wire at `regions_get.go:70`), so `buildRegionTree`/`flattenVisible` have no data to prune non-downloadable leaves or the group ancestors that exist solely to reach them.
- **resolved product decision:** filter to `enabled == true` ONLY. A leaf with `enabled != true` (false or absent) is removed; a group left with zero surviving children after pruning is removed. An `enabled == true` leaf that is still `status == "building"` MUST keep rendering (matches the existing disabled-row treatment). Do NOT additionally gate on build status.
