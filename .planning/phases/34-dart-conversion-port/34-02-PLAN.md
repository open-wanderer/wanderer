<objective>
Create the `moving_duration` field end to end — PocketBase schema, published OpenAPI contract,
TypeScript model, Dart model, ObjectBox entity, the app's multipart save body — and implement
D-10's display rule (`moving_duration` when present, else `duration`) on the web through one
shared helper.

Purpose: D-10 is the load-bearing decision of this phase. `updateTotals()` in
`web/src/routes/trail/edit/[id]/+page.svelte` recomputes all four GPX metrics from the route on
~14 mutation paths. Moving time is not recoverable from a GPX, so storing it in `duration` would
make any anchor nudge, undo, crop or reverse silently and permanently destroy it. A separate
field makes that destruction structurally impossible instead of relying on every future writer
to remember a guard.

Output: a new PocketBase migration, `moving_duration` on all three Trail-shaped OpenAPI schemas,
`Trail.moving_duration` in TS, `Trail.movingDuration` / `TrailEntity.movingDuration` in Dart, the
field on the app's trail form body, and `trailDisplayDuration()` wired into the web's five Trail
duration display sites. No writer of `moving_duration` exists yet — plan 34-05 supplies the
value from the recording session.
</objective>
