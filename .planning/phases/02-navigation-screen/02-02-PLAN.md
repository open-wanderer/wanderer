<objective>
Build the full-screen `NavigationScreen` (the core deliverable of this phase) and wire the `/trail/:id/navigate` go_router sub-route. The screen composes a fresh `FlutterMap` (modeled on `map_screen.dart`, NOT `WandererMap` — research anti-pattern), drives it with one shared GPS stream that feeds both the `CurrentLocationLayer` (follow + heading) and the Plan-01 `Navigation` notifier (advancement + breadcrumb), renders the maneuver banner, compass toggle, exit, recenter, and the crimson breadcrumb polyline.

After this plan, a user who opens `/trail/:id/navigate` with a `NavigateResponse` can follow the trail end-to-end on a live map with auto-advancing instructions — the complete navigation experience minus the entry buttons (Plan 03).

Purpose: Delivers NAV-03 (follow map), NAV-04 (maneuver banner), NAV-05 (orientation toggle), NAV-06 (auto-advance), NAV-07 (exit), NAV-08 (breadcrumb) as one vertical slice — a working screen, not a layer.
Output: `navigation_screen.dart`, the `navigate` route in `router_provider.dart`, and all nav i18n keys in both `.arb` files.
</objective>
