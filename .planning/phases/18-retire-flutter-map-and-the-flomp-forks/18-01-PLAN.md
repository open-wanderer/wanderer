<objective>
Prepare the Flutter codebase for a clean removal of the six map packages by
severing every remaining source-level dependency on them, WHILE the packages are
still installed so `flutter analyze` diagnoses code correctness rather than
missing-dependency errors (RESEARCH "code before manifest" rationale). Three real
blockers are closed here: (1) relocate the live `effectiveBrightness()` function
out of the otherwise-dead `map_style_provider.dart`; (2) replace the two
`flutter_map_location_marker` plain data classes with file-local equivalents;
(3) delete the confirmed-dead files that import the flomp forks and bare
`flutter_map`.

Purpose: This is the load-bearing prerequisite for CLEAN-01 and CLEAN-02 — the
pubspec removal in Plan 02 fails a whole-package analyze unless every importer is
severed first. A naive "just delete the pubspec lines" plan breaks three importers
of `effectiveBrightness`, three importers of `LocationMarkerPosition`, and the
`tool/` extraction CLI.

Output: A codebase that compiles clean under a whole-package `flutter analyze` and
green `flutter test` with the six packages still in `pubspec.yaml` — nothing left
importing them.
</objective>
