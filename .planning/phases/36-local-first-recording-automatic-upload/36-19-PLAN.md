<objective>
Close the three mechanically-fixable hygiene warnings, and convert the one that cannot be
mechanically fixed into tracked work rather than silence:

- **WR-11** — `TrailPanel`'s `TabBar` is gated on `!trail.isLocal`, and `TrailEntity.toModel()`
  hardcodes `isLocal: true` for **every** row, downloaded trails included. Summit logs and
  comments are therefore unreachable on any trail served from ObjectBox, including the case
  `trail_dropdown.dart:361-365` calls out by name: `TrailNotifier.build()` falls back to the
  cache on *any* fetch exception, so one timeout on your own downloaded trail hides its comments
  until the provider refetches. `trail_dropdown.dart:48-53` already documents that `isLocal`
  stopped being a usable "device-only" signal in this phase and switched to `isUnsynced`;
  `trail_panel.dart` was edited in the same plan and was not brought onto the same footing.
  The review records this as pre-existing (Phase 35's `21c4b1ee`) rather than introduced here —
  it is fixed anyway because the fix is two lines, the predicate it needs already exists and is
  already the convention in the sibling file, and the trails this phase creates are exactly the
  ones that make the wrong gate visible.
- **WR-07** — `retry_upload` appears in the template ARB and in all 13 locales' untranslated
  report but has no reference anywhere in `app/lib` or `app/test`. The retry affordance uses
  `sync_failed` as both label and action. It inflates the translation backlog by one string in 13
  locales for nothing.
- **WR-12** — `LocalTrailMetrics` is documented as "three parallel lists", but
  `PropertyQuery<double>.find()` excludes nulls unless `replaceNullWith` is supplied
  (objectbox 5.3.1) and all three columns are nullable, so the lists have different lengths and
  no positional correspondence. Harmless today because only per-axis maxima are taken — and
  actively dangerous the moment someone follows the comment's invitation to zip them.
- **WR-06 — DEFERRED, not fixed.** `untranslated_messages.json` lists 13 locales missing 13
  keys, two of which are the copy on irreversible actions. This needs real translations. Machine
  translation is explicitly out of scope for this batch, and no autonomous agent can supply
  correct destructive-action copy in 13 languages. This plan records the exact work list as a
  tracked todo instead of pretending it is closed.

Purpose: stop a downloaded trail hiding half its content, stop the backlog lying about its size,
stop a doc comment inviting a data-corrupting change, and stop the translation gap being
invisible.
Output: one predicate governing the tab gate, a backlog that matches reality, and a named
handoff.
</objective>
