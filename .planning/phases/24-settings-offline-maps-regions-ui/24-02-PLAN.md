Build the Offline Maps/Regions Settings screen and wire it into navigation — the single user-facing deliverable of Phase 24. Purpose: Give the user one place to discover, download, pause/resume, delete, DEM-toggle, and monitor offline regions — the first consumer of the whole v1.6 download stack.
Output: `settings_offline_regions_screen.dart` (new); a Settings entry `ListTile`; a `/settings/regions` `GoRoute`.
</objective>

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
