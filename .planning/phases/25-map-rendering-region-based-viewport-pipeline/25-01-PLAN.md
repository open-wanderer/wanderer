<objective>
Resolve RENDER-03 — the milestone's highest-risk unknown — before any of Phase 25's rewiring commits to a composition strategy. Build a throwaway on-device harness that materializes 10-20 duplicated region vector/DEM style sources+layers (the realistic upper bound for a trail spanning several downloaded regions × the basemap's per-cell layer duplication) and lets a human measure, on a mid-tier Android device, whether the recommended full-style-reload region-swap path (`controller.setStyle(json)`) has acceptable flicker/perf, or whether the phase must reach for incremental `addSource`/`removeSource`.

RESEARCH.md already resolved the *API-availability* half of RENDER-03 to HIGH confidence via direct pub-cache source read (incremental `addSource`/`removeSource`/`addLayer`/`removeLayer` are real, non-stub native calls on both platforms, and `navigation_screen.dart` already uses them in production for its breadcrumb layer). What it explicitly could NOT resolve — and what ROADMAP success criterion 1 requires — is the empirical 10-20-duplicated-source rendering/perf measurement on real hardware. This plan delivers that measurement and settles the strategy via a `checkpoint:decision`.

Purpose: De-risk the composition strategy before Wave 2 wires TrailMap/navigation_screen, per the ROADMAP risk gate ("Resolve this first, before investing in the rest of the phase's rewiring").
Output: A standalone harness under `app/test/` (never in production routes, mirroring the 23-06 `tile_repository_manager_harness.dart` precedent) plus a settled composition-strategy decision.
</objective>
