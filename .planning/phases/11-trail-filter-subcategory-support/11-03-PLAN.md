<objective>
Add subcategory support to the full TrailFilterScreen: switch category chip labels to locale-resolved display names, render FontAwesome icon avatars on category chips, and add an `AnimatedSize`-wrapped "Subcategories" section (below Category) that appears only when ≥1 category is selected, shows subcategory chips scoped to the selected categories with primary+badge icon avatars, and omits categories/subcategories the user has hidden in preferences.

Consumes `TrailFilter.subcategory` (Plan 01), `WandererFilterChip.avatarBuilder` + `l10n.subcategories` (Plan 02), and the Phase 10 providers/display extensions.

Output: a TrailFilterScreen where selecting categories reveals a scoped, preference-filtered, icon-bearing subcategory chip section whose selections flow into the active TrailFilter.
</objective>
