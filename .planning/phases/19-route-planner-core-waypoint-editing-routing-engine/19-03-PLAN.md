<objective>
Build the two native-map rendering surfaces the route planner needs: an interactive `WidgetLayer` of numbered, draggable route-anchor markers (`RouteAnchorLayer`), and a native GL `StyleController`-driven segment renderer (`RouteSegmentLayer`) that draws the three mutually-exclusive segment visual states (routed/straight/blocked) plus an invisible wide hit-test layer for reliable segment-tap detection.

Purpose: These are the visual/interactive surfaces 19-04's screen embeds and wires taps into. Building them against 19-02's already-defined `RouteAnchors` provider contract (rather than against assumptions) means 19-04 only has to wire gestures, not invent rendering.
Output: `route_anchor_layer.dart`, `route_segment_layer.dart`, and an extended `route_segment_util.dart` with a unit-tested pure GeoJSON builder.
</objective>
