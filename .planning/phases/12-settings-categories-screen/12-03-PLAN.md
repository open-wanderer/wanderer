<objective>
Build `SettingsSubcategoriesScreen` — the leaf screen reached by tapping a category row. It shows the parent category's subcategories with per-row visibility toggle (auto-save), drag-handle reorder scoped to the parent category, an empty state for categories with no subcategories, and the own-trail confirm-before-disable dialog for subcategories. Consumes the Plan 01 `reorder` method, own-trail count helper, and l10n keys. Mirrors the sibling category screen's structure (D-06).

Purpose: Deliver SETCAT-08 (per-subcategory switches on a dedicated screen), SETCAT-10 (subcategory reorder POST scoped to parent), and the subcategory half of SETCAT-11 (own-trail confirm dialog).

Output: `app/lib/routes/settings_subcategories_screen.dart` (new `ConsumerStatefulWidget`).
</objective>
