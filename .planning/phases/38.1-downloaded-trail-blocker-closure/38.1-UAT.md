## Tests

### 1. Cold Start Smoke Test
expected: Kill the app completely and relaunch. App boots without errors, the startup sweep completes, the library list loads with every downloaded trail still present, and trail photos still render (not broken thumbnails).
result: pass

### 2. Library Screen Reachable, No Dead Route
expected: Open the library. It lists downloaded trails. Tapping one opens the normal trail detail screen. No blank screen, no navigation error, no "route not found".
result: pass

### 3. Remove Download on an Ordinary Downloaded Trail
expected: On a downloaded trail you do NOT own and are not recording, the dropdown offers "Remove download". Confirming removes the offline copy — the trail stays visible (now not-offline, offering Download again) and is NOT deleted from the server. Device storage for that trail is reclaimed.
result: pass

### 4. Remove Download Hidden for Your Own Live Capture
expected: For a trail that is your own not-yet-uploaded recording AND is in your offline library, "Remove download" does NOT appear — neither in the trail dropdown nor in the library screen's long-press menu. The action is hidden, not greyed out.
result: pass

### 5. Delete Your Own Unsynced Capture
expected: Delete a trail you recorded that has not uploaded yet. It disappears from your lists, its photos are removed from device storage, and you get a success confirmation — not an error toast.
result: pass

### 6. Delete Immediately After an Upload Completes
expected: Delete a recorded trail at the moment its deferred upload finishes (leave the detail screen open while the drain runs, then delete). It deletes successfully. You do NOT get "error deleting trail" for a trail that actually went away.
result: pass

### 7. Edit a Trail Where One Waypoint Fails to Save
expected: With an offline copy of a trail that has several waypoints, edit it under conditions where at least one waypoint save fails (e.g. drop connectivity mid-save). The offline copy KEEPS all its existing waypoints — none silently disappear from the downloaded copy.
result: pass

### 8. Account Switch Does Not Leak the Previous Account's Trail
expected: While signed in as account A, open one of A's not-yet-uploaded recordings. Without force-quitting, sign out and sign in as account B, then navigate back. B does not see A's unsynced trail, and B is not offered Delete on it.
result: pass

### 9. Cross-Account Isolation of Unsynced Photos
expected: With account A holding an un-uploaded recording with photos, and account B holding the same trail in its offline library, B removing/deleting it does NOT destroy A's un-uploaded photos. Signing back in as A shows the recording and its photos intact.
result: pass
