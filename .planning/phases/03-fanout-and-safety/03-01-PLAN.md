Add the `instanceFollowerInboxes(app core.App) ([]string, error)` helper to `db/federation/activity.go`. This is the single source of instance-follower inbox URLs that every outgoing fanout function in Plan 02 (create.go) and Plan 03 (delete.go) appends to its recipient list.

Purpose: Without this helper, no outgoing Create/Update/Delete activity can reach instance-actor followers. It is the foundation of SYNC-01/02/03 and must be startup-safe (D-02) so content hooks firing before the instance actor is seeded never error.

</objective>
