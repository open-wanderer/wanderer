---
title: Statistics
description: Where can I find statistics and how are they derived?
---

<span class="-tracking-[0.075em]">wanderer</span> can show you a wide range of useful statistics about your latest adventures. Head over to the profile page (`/profile`) to get started. The statistics include your [summit logs](/use/summit-logs) as well as trails explicitly marked as completed. A completed trail is counted separately only when it has no summit logs; otherwise its summit logs provide the activity dates and metrics. Add GPS data to a summit log or trail to include detailed distance and elevation values.

![Statistics](../../../assets/guides/wanderer_stats.gif)

With the filters in the top right corner you can determine which activities should be included in the statistics. Moving to another month in the calendar updates this date range automatically. With the drop-down menu above the activity bar chart you can determine which metric should be visualized in the time series graph.

In the table at the bottom of the page you see an overview of all activities that match your selected filters. Clicking on the route preview at the beginning of each row will display the route on a more detailed map so you can take a closer at all the relevant data.
