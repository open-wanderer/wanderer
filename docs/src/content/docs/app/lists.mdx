Lists group trails into collections — a multi-day trek, a summer bucket list, your local favorites. They are the same [lists](/use/lists) you know from the web interface.

## Browsing lists

<PhoneGrid src={listsShot} alt="The Lists tab">

The **Lists** tab shows your lists and lets you search and sort them. Each card sums up the whole collection: how many trails it holds, and their combined distance, duration, and elevation gain and loss.

Tap a list to open it: you'll see its description and every trail it contains, each leading to its [trail details](/app/trail-details).

</PhoneGrid>

## List map

A list can also be viewed on a map, showing all of its trails together — handy for getting an overview of a multi-stage route or planning which trail of an area to tackle next.
