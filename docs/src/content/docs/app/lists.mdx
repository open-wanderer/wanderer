Lists group trails into collections: a multi-day trek, a summer bucket list, your local favorites. They are the same [lists](/use/lists) you know from the web interface.

In the beta the app lets you **browse** lists and open their trails. Creating a list, adding or removing trails, and sharing a list are done on the web for now; see [what works where](/app/what-works-where#lists). Lists need a connection; they aren't stored on the device.

## Browsing lists

<PhoneGrid src={listsShot} alt="The Lists tab">

The **Lists** tab shows your lists and lets you search them and sort them by name or creation date. Each card sums up the whole collection: how many trails it holds, and their combined distance, duration, and elevation gain and loss.

Tap a list to open it: you'll see its description and every trail it contains, each leading to its [trail details](/app/trail-details).

</PhoneGrid>

## List map

A list can also be viewed on a map, showing all of its trails together, handy for getting an overview of a multi-stage route or deciding which trail of an area to tackle next.
