## The map

<PhoneGrid src={mapShot} alt="The trails map with a selected trail">

The **Trails** tab shows an interactive map of your surroundings. Trails are displayed as markers, automatically grouped into numbered clusters when many trails are close together. Tap a cluster to zoom in, tap a marker to select the trail. The selected trail's route is drawn on the map and a preview card shows its key stats; tap the card to open the full [trail details](/app/trail-details), or swipe it down to deselect.

Pan or zoom to a new area and tap **Search this area** to load the trails there. The **crosshair** button centers the map on your position.

The panel at the bottom holds the same trails as a list. Drag it up to browse them, and tap **Map** to collapse it again.

Browsing trails needs a connection. Offline, the map shows only the trails stored on your device; see [Offline use](/app/offline-use).

</PhoneGrid>

## Search

<PhoneGrid src={searchShot} alt="Searching across trails, locations and users" side="left">

The search screen searches your instance for trails, lists, locations, and users, including users on other instances yours is connected to. Use the chips at the top (**All**, **Trails**, **Lists**, **Locations**, **Users**) to narrow the search to one result type, and tap a location result to jump the map to that place.

</PhoneGrid>

## Filters

<PhoneGrid src={filterShot} alt="The trail filter screen">

The **Filter** button opens a full filter screen. You can restrict results by:

- **Categories & subcategories** (e.g. hiking, cycling)
- **Tags**
- **Author**
- **Visibility**: public, private, or trails other users shared with you
- **Difficulty**: easy, moderate, difficult
- **Distance, elevation gain & loss**: range sliders
- **Before / After**: a date range
- **Like status**: trails you liked
- **Completion status**: completed, not completed, or no preference

Active filters are shown as a badge on the **Filter** button; the icon in the top right of the filter screen clears them all at once. A quick-filter bar above the map gives one-tap access to category, difficulty, sort order, and **Available offline**, which shows only trails stored on your device.

</PhoneGrid>

## Sorting

The **Sort** button orders results by name, creation date, date, difficulty, distance, duration, elevation gain, elevation loss, or likes, ascending or descending. **Date** is the day the trail was hiked; **Creation date** is when it was added to the instance.
