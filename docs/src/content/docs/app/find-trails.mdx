## The map

<PhoneGrid src={mapShot} alt="The trails map with a selected trail">

The **Trails** tab shows an interactive map of your surroundings. Trails are displayed as markers, automatically clustered when many trails are close together — tap a cluster to zoom in, tap a marker to select the trail. The selected trail's route is drawn on the map and a preview panel shows its key stats; tap the panel to open the full [trail details](/app/trail-details).

Pan or zoom to a new area and tap **Search this area** to load the trails there. You can also switch between the map and a list view of the current results.

</PhoneGrid>

## Search

<PhoneGrid src={searchShot} alt="Searching across trails, locations and users" side="left">

The search screen searches your instance for trails, locations, lists, and users — including users on other instances your instance federates with. Use the chips at the top to narrow the search to one result type, and tap a location result to jump the map to that place.

</PhoneGrid>

## Filters

<PhoneGrid src={filterShot} alt="The trail filter screen">

The **Filter** button opens a full filter screen. You can restrict results by:

- **Category & subcategory** (e.g. hiking, cycling)
- **Difficulty** — easy, moderate, difficult
- **Distance, elevation gain & loss** — range sliders
- **Completion status** — whether you have completed the trail
- **Like status** — trails you liked
- **Visibility status** — public, private, or shared trails
- **Date range** and **tags**
- **Author**

Active filters are shown as a badge on the **Filter** button; the icon in the top right of the filter screen clears them all at once. A quick-filter bar above the map gives one-tap access to the most common filters, including **Available offline** to show only trails stored on your device.

</PhoneGrid>

## Sorting

The **Sort** button orders results by criteria such as creation date, distance, elevation gain, or difficulty — ascending or descending.
