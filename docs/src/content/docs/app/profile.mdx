The **Profile** tab shows your own profile: avatar, handle, join date, and bio, followed by counters for followers, following, trails, and lists. Below that come your lists and a feed of your recent activity. Other users' profiles look the same; open them from search results or by tapping a trail's author.

## Trails & lists

Tapping the **Trails** counter opens the profile's trails, which can be browsed as a list or all together on a map. The **Lists** counter does the same for its lists. Your own profile additionally shows **Trails (on device)**, everything stored on this phone.

## Following

The **Followers** and **Following** counters open the respective lists. On another user's profile, tap **Follow** next to their counters to stay up to date with their newest public trails, including users on other instances yours is connected to. Tap again (**Following**) to unfollow.

</PhoneGrid>

## Share your profile

<PhoneGrid src={shareShot} alt="Sharing your profile via QR code" side="left">

The share icon at the top opens a **QR code** for your profile, along with the profile link itself. Anyone who scans the code with their phone camera lands on your profile in their browser. **Copy Link** puts the link on your clipboard, and **Share** hands it to any app on your device. Great for exchanging profiles on the trail.

</PhoneGrid>

## Settings

The gear icon opens the [app settings](/app/settings).

Viewing other profiles, followers and the feed needs a connection; offline, your own profile shows only the trails stored on this device.
