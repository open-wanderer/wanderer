The <span class="-tracking-[0.075em]">wanderer</span> mobile app is currently in **beta**. Expect rough edges and occasional bugs. Under no circumstances should you rely on the app as your sole means of navigation during a hike — always carry a backup, such as a paper map and compass or a dedicated GPS device.

<PhoneGrid src={profileShot} alt="Your profile">

The **Profile** tab shows your own profile: avatar, handle, join date, and bio, followed by counters for followers, following, trails, and lists. Below that come your lists and a feed of your recent activity. Other users' profiles look the same — open them from search results or from a trail's author.

### Trails & lists

Tapping the **Trails** counter opens the profile's trails, which can be browsed as a list or all together on a map. The **Lists** counter does the same for its lists. On your own profile you'll also find the trails currently stored on this device.

### Following

The **Followers** and **Following** counters open the respective lists. Follow other users to stay up to date with their newest public trails — including users on other instances if your instance federates.

</PhoneGrid>

## Share your profile

<PhoneGrid src={shareShot} alt="Sharing your profile via QR code" side="left">

The share icon at the top opens a **QR code** that leads to your profile when scanned, along with the profile link itself. **Copy Link** puts it on your clipboard, and **Share** hands it to any app on your device. Great for exchanging profiles on the trail.

</PhoneGrid>
