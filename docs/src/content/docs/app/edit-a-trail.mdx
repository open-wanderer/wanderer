Whichever way a trail starts, [recorded](/app/record-a-trail), [planned](/app/plan-a-route), or [imported](/app/record-a-trail#importing-a-file), it ends up in the trail editor before it is saved. The same editor opens when you choose **Edit** on one of your own trails.

## Basic info

- **Name** and **Description**: the description supports simple formatting (bold, italic, lists, quotes, links).
- **Location**: a place name for the trail, free text.
- **Date**: the day of the hike, used for the *Date* sort order and date filters.
- **Tags**: free-form keywords other users can filter by.
- **Category** and **Difficulty** (easy, moderate, difficult). A recording or planned route pre-selects the category from the travel profile you chose.
- **Public / Private**: whether other users can see the trail. New trails start with the default from your [privacy settings](/app/settings#privacy).
- **Completed / Not completed**: marks the trail as one you have done, for the *Completion status* filter. It does not create a summit log; those are written on the web for now.

## Photos & videos

Add photos and videos from your device. They are uploaded together with the trail; if some fail, the trail is still saved and the app tells you how many photos are missing. Choosing the cover image is done on the web for now.

## Waypoints

Waypoints are points of interest along the trail: a viewpoint, a hut, a junction.

- **Create waypoint** opens a form with name, icon, description, coordinates, and photos. The waypoint's position along the trail is worked out from its coordinates.
- **From Photos** lets you pick photos from your device; every photo with GPS coordinates in it becomes a waypoint at that spot, with the photo attached. Photos without location data are skipped ("No GPS data found in this photo").

Tap a waypoint in the list to edit or remove it.

## Route

**Edit route** (the pencil on the map preview) opens the [planner](/app/plan-a-route) with the trail's route as anchors. Move, add or delete anchors and confirm with the checkmark to bring the changed route back into the editor.

## Saving and offline

**Save** stores the trail on your device first and uploads it to your instance right away. Without a connection the trail waits in your [Library](/app/offline-use#the-library) and uploads automatically later.

Editing an existing trail that is already on your instance needs a connection, because the changes are made to the server copy. If you leave the editor with unsaved changes, the app asks whether to discard them.
