:::caution[Beta]
The <span class="-tracking-[0.075em]">wanderer</span> mobile app is currently in **beta**. Expect rough edges and occasional bugs. Under no circumstances should you rely on the app as your sole means of navigation during a hike — always carry a backup, such as a paper map and compass or a dedicated GPS device.
:::

Tap **Navigate** on any [trail's detail page](/app/trail-details) to follow it step by step.

## On the trail

The navigation screen shows the trail and your live position on the map. The map follows you in heading-up mode — it rotates so that your direction of travel points up; a compass button resets the map to north. Upcoming maneuvers are announced with their distance ("In 300 m …"), and the app notifies you when you reach the end of the trail.

Alongside the map you can keep an eye on your live stats — distance covered, speed, time in motion — and the trail's elevation profile with your current position on it.

Navigation keeps running in the background with a persistent notification, so your screen can stay off between turns.

## Recording while navigating

Your movement along the trail is tracked, so when you finish (or stop early) you can save your actual track as a trail of your own — with the same **Save recording** options as a free [recording](/app/record-a-trail).

## Offline navigation

Navigation works without a network connection for trails stored on your device: download the trail (and ideally its map region) beforehand — see [Offline use](/app/offline-use). Your position comes from GPS, which needs no internet.
