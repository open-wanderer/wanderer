Open the settings via the gear icon on your [profile](/app/profile). All account-related settings are synchronized with your instance — a change made in the app also applies on the web, and vice versa.

The screen is split into seven sections:

- **My Account** — avatar, bio, email, password
- **Privacy** — who can see your profile, trails, and lists
- **Language & Units** — app language, metric or imperial
- **Notifications** — which events reach you
- **Categories** — reorder or hide trail categories
- **Offline Maps/Regions** — manage downloaded map data
- **Appearance** — light, dark, or system theme

</PhoneGrid>

## My Account

Manage your account: change your avatar and bio, update your email address or password, and — if you must — delete your account. Deleting your account removes all your data from your instance and cannot be undone.

## Privacy

Control who sees what:

- **Account privacy** — a public profile appears in search results and can be followed; a private profile is visible only to you, while you can still publish individual trails or lists.
- **Default trail visibility** — whether newly created trails start out public or private.
- **Default list visibility** — the same for lists.

Visibility of individual trails and lists can always be changed later, per item.

## Language & Units

Choose the app's language (14 languages available) and switch between metric and imperial units. The unit setting applies everywhere — trail stats, navigation, elevation profiles.

## Notifications

Choose which events notify you — new followers, comments, summit logs on your trails, trail shares, and more — separately for web and email notifications.

## Categories

Personalize your instance's trail [categories](/use/categories): reorder them by priority, and hide categories (and subcategories) you don't use. Disabling a category you still have trails in asks for confirmation first and lets you jump straight to those trails.

## Offline Maps/Regions

Manage your downloaded map regions — see [Offline use](/app/offline-use).

## Appearance

Switch between light, dark, or system theme.
