Tap the **➕** button in the center of the bottom bar to open the **New Trail** screen. You can choose between three sources:

- **Open trail planner** — draw a new route on the map ([Plan a route](/app/plan-a-route))
- **Record trail** — track your live coordinates and log your journey in real time
- **Import file** — upload GPX, KML, KMZ, TCX or FIT files directly from your device storage

</PhoneGrid>

## Recording

Choose **Record trail** and pick a travel profile (hiking or one of the biking profiles). The app needs permission to access your location — including in the background, so the recording continues while your screen is off or you use other apps. A persistent notification shows that location tracking is active.

While recording, the screen shows your track on the map along with live stats: distance, elevation gain and loss, speed, average speed, and time in motion. You can **pause** and **resume** at any time, and leave the recording screen — an active recording survives app restarts and can be resumed from where you left off.

## Saving

When you stop the recording, the **Save recording** options let you polish the track before it becomes a trail:

- **Follow roads** — snap the recorded path to the nearest roads and trails
- **Recalculate heights** — replace recorded GPS elevation with more accurate values from the map

Afterwards, the trail editor opens where you can add a name, description, category, photos, and waypoints before saving.

## Recording offline

Recording works entirely without a network connection. The recording and the finished trail are stored on your device first and appear in your [Library](/app/offline-use). As soon as your instance is reachable again, the app uploads the trail automatically — the sync status chip on the trail shows the current state ("Waiting to upload", "Upload failed · Tap to retry").

To also have a base map while recording offline, download the region beforehand — see [Offline use](/app/offline-use).
