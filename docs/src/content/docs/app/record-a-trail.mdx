Tap the **plus** button in the middle of the bottom bar to open the **New Trail** screen. You can choose between three sources:

- **Open trail planner**: draw a new route on the map ([Plan a route](/app/plan-a-route)); needs a connection
- **Record trail**: track your live position and log your journey in real time
- **Import file**: load a GPX, KML, KMZ, TCX or FIT file from your device storage (see [Importing a file](#importing-a-file))

## Recording

Choose **Record trail** and pick a travel profile: **Hiking**, or one of the biking profiles (**Biking / Hybrid**, **Mountain**, **Cross**, **Road**). The profile pre-selects the trail's category when you save and, if you choose **Follow roads**, decides whether the track is snapped to footpaths or to cycling routes.

### Allow location access in the background

A recording should keep going while your screen is off or you use another app. For that, the app needs permission to use your location **in the background**. A normal "while using the app" permission is not enough and the recording will stop when the screen locks.

- **Android** can't grant this from within the app. The first time you record, the app explains this and offers to open the system settings. There, choose **Permissions → Location → Allow all the time**. If you skipped the prompt, open your phone's **Settings → Apps → wanderer → Permissions → Location** and set it to *Allow all the time* yourself; the app only asks once.
- **iOS** shows a system prompt asking to change location access to **Always**. Choose *Change to Always Allow*.

A persistent notification ("Recording your trail") shows that location tracking is active.

### While recording

The screen shows your track on the map along with live stats: distance, elevation gain and loss, speed, average speed, and time in motion. You can **pause** and **resume** at any time, and leave the recording screen; an active recording survives app restarts. If you open the app while a recording is running, it asks "Resume recording?"; choose yes to get back to it.

## Saving

Tap the **stop** button and the app asks "Stop recording?":

- **Cancel** keeps recording.
- **Exit** stops and **discards** the recording; there is no undo.
- **Save track** stops and continues to the save options below. It only appears once you have recorded enough to be worth saving.

The **Save recording** options let you polish the track before it becomes a trail:

- **Follow roads**: snap the recorded path to the nearest roads and trails
- **Recalculate heights**: replace recorded GPS elevation with more accurate values from the map

Both run on your instance, so they are only offered while you have a connection. Afterwards, the trail editor opens where you add a name, description, category, photos, and waypoints before saving.

## Recording offline

Recording works entirely without a network connection. The recording and the finished trail are stored on your device first and appear in your [Library](/app/offline-use#the-library). As soon as your instance is reachable again, the app uploads the trail automatically; the sync status chip on the trail shows the current state ("Waiting to upload", "Uploading…", "Upload failed · Tap to retry").

Two things are different offline:

- The **Save recording** options are skipped; the track is saved exactly as recorded. You can still edit the trail later.
- Until the trail has uploaded, its detail page only offers **Edit**; navigating and downloading become available once it is on your instance.

To also have a base map while recording offline, download the region beforehand; see [Offline use](/app/offline-use).

## Importing a file

**Import file** opens your device's file picker. GPX files are read directly on the device and work offline. KML, KMZ, TCX and FIT files are converted by your instance first, so they need a connection. Offline, the app tells you "Only GPX files can be imported offline".

After the file is read, the same two options as above appear under the name **Adjust track** (again only while online), and the trail editor opens with the imported route.
