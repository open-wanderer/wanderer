The messages below are quoted as the app shows them. If yours isn't here, or the fix doesn't help, [report it on GitHub](https://github.com/open-wanderer/wanderer/issues/new?template=app_bug_report.yml); the template asks for everything we need.

## Connecting and logging in

**The app can't reach my instance.**
Type the address without anything in front of it (`wanderer.example.com`); the app adds `https://` itself. Instances must be reachable over HTTPS; connections to a plain `http://` address are blocked by the phone. If the address is right and the web interface works in your browser, check the next entry.

**I can log in, but the map stays empty and most things fail.**
The instance is running a version that doesn't support the app yet. Ask your administrator to follow [Mobile app support](/run/backend-configuration/mobile-app).

**Login fails with the right password.**
Log in on the web once to rule out the account itself, then try again. If your instance uses a third-party login (Google, GitHub, …), use the same button in the app.

**I want to use a different instance or account.**
Log out from the bottom of the [settings](/app/settings#log-out). The welcome screen lets you pick another instance.

## Location and recording

**"Location services are disabled. Please enable GPS to use navigation."**
Location is switched off at the system level. Turn it on in your phone's quick settings or under Settings → Location.

**"Location permission is required for navigation."** / **"Location permission is permanently denied. Please enable it in Settings."**
Open your phone's Settings → Apps → wanderer → Permissions → Location and allow it. On Android choose **Allow all the time** so recordings continue with the screen off; on iOS choose **Always**.

**My recording stopped when the screen locked.**
Background location isn't allowed. See [Allow location access in the background](/app/record-a-trail#allow-location-access-in-the-background). The app only asks once; after that the permission has to be changed in the system settings.

**"Unable to determine your location. Please try again."**
The phone hasn't got a GPS fix yet. Step outside or away from tall buildings and wait a few seconds. Indoors, GPS can take a long time or fail entirely.

**"Couldn't start navigation. Check your connection and try again."**
Turn-by-turn directions are fetched from your instance when you start navigating a trail that isn't stored on the device. Either connect, or [download the trail](/app/offline-use#download-trails) while you still have a connection; the directions are saved with it.

## Trails and files

**"Only GPX files can be imported offline."**
KML, KMZ, TCX and FIT files are converted by your instance. Connect, or convert the file to GPX first.

**"Could not import file"** / **"Error reading file"**
The file couldn't be read as a track. Check that it opens on the web; if it does, [file a bug](https://github.com/open-wanderer/wanderer/issues/new?template=app_bug_report.yml) and attach the file (renamed to `.txt`).

**"Editing works on the server copy of this trail. Connect to the internet to edit it."** / **"This trail is already on the server. Connect to the internet to delete it."**
The trail lives on your instance; changes and deletions are made there. Connect and try again.

**"This trail is uploading right now. Wait for the upload to finish, then try again."**
A recording or import is being sent to your instance. Wait for the sync chip to disappear.

**"This trail is no longer on this device."**
The device copy was removed (for example after logging out and in with another account). Download it again from its detail page.

**The sync chip says "Upload failed · Tap to retry".**
The last upload attempt didn't go through. Tap the chip once you have a connection. If it keeps failing, the trail may be larger than your instance allows (photos!). Try removing photos, or ask your administrator about the [upload limits](/run/backend-configuration/adjust-filesize-limits).

**"Trail saved, but some waypoints failed to save."** / **"Trail saved, but N photos couldn't be saved."**
The trail itself is on your instance. Open it, choose **Edit**, and add the missing waypoints or photos again.

## Offline maps

**"No offline regions available"** / **"Ask your Wanderer instance administrator to configure downloadable regions."**
Your instance hasn't enabled any regions. Point your administrator to the [region catalogue](/run/backend-configuration/region-catalogue) page.

**A region is greyed out with "Not yet available".**
Your instance has enabled the region but is still preparing its map files. This can take a while for large regions; check back later.

**A region shows "Build failed".**
The instance couldn't prepare the region. Your administrator can see why in the server logs.

**"Download map data first"** on the elevation data row.
Elevation data can only be added to a region whose **Vector** map is already downloaded.

**"Can't load regions"** / **"Connect to the internet to browse and manage downloadable regions."**
The list of regions comes from your instance. Regions you already downloaded keep working offline.

**The trail shows on an empty background.**
The trail is downloaded, but the map region around it isn't. See [Download map regions](/app/offline-use#download-map-regions).

## Navigation

**The map doesn't rotate with me.**
That's the default. Tap the **compass** to switch to heading-up mode; see [Navigate a trail](/app/navigate-a-trail#on-the-trail).

**I left the trail and the directions don't change.**
The app doesn't reroute in the beta; it keeps pointing at the next turn of the original trail. Use the map to get back onto it.

**I don't hear any directions.**
There is no voice guidance in the beta. Directions are shown in the banner at the top of the screen.
